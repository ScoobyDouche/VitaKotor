A small release on top of v0.1.9.1,
with one fix you will actually hear.

## Cutscene voice lines come back

In a 38-minute session, six spoken
lines simply did not play. The
scene ran on in silence for as long
as the line should have lasted, then
carried on as if it had been
delivered.

The decoder was asking for far more
memory than the clip needed -- 1881
KB for a line that fits in 828 --
and when the heap could not serve
that, the audio was replaced with
silence of the right length. The
oversized request came from assuming
the worst possible case for every
frame in the file. KOTOR's voice is
48 kbps mono, which is nothing like
the worst case, and the file says so
in its own header. The buffer is now
sized from that, with slack left for
variable bitrate.

Both failures in the log that
prompted this fit comfortably
afterwards. As a side effect the
large-allocation pool goes further
too, since these were among the
biggest requests being made.

## Known issues

Two faults remain, each seen exactly
once in about four hours of play,
and neither reproduced since. They
are listed because you may hit them,
not because they are reliable.

**Input can stop responding.** Seen
once, around 50 minutes in: the
camera stick and the touchscreen
both stopped doing anything, while
the game carried on running and
drawing at full speed. Quitting to
the LiveArea and relaunching clears
it. The game was still being handed
input at the time and simply stopped
acting on it. This release adds
logging to catch it in the act; it
is the thing being worked on.

**World geometry can tear into
diagonal streaks.** Seen once at
about 44 minutes: walls and floors
smear, while the HUD and dialogue
text keep drawing perfectly. It
followed the player between areas.
A later 52-minute session was
completely clean. Nothing crashes
and your save is safe.

## Installing

You need your own copy of the
**Android** release of KOTOR: the
`.apk` and both `.obb` files.
Nothing here contains game code or
data. See the README for the full
setup, including `kubridge.skprx`
and `libshacccg.suprx`, both of
which are required.

Save often. It's worth playing in
sessions now; it's not yet a
playthrough.
