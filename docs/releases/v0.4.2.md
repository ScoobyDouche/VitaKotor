# VitaKotor v0.4.2 — griff comes home

A fix release. Griff now turns up in the Sand People Enclave, texture quality can be picked from the MODS button, and busy areas no longer run out of sound channels.

## What changed

- **Griff spawns in the Sand People Enclave even if you opened their chests first.** This bug is in the original game. The Enclave remembers "Griff has been spawned" and "you were warned about stealing" in the same switch, so opening one of their chests before asking Czerka about Griff made the game think he was already there. The port now patches the two chest scripts in memory as the game loads them, so the warning uses its own switch. No game files are shipped, and a mod that replaces those scripts is left untouched.

- **Texture quality is selectable from the MODS button.** After choosing a mod set, a second list offers **16-bit, faster** or **32-bit, sharper**. The choice is saved and applies on that same boot. 16-bit stays the default. It can also be set by hand as `TextureBits=16` or `TextureBits=32` in the `[Vita Options]` section of `swkotor.ini`.

- **Busy areas no longer run out of sound channels.** The game asked for 40 channels, and crowded fights could fill all of them. When that happened, a line of dialogue could replay over and over with no one talking. The port now gives the game 96 channels. Across about an hour of play the most in use at once was 28.

- **KOTOR-nolog.vpk**: an optional build with diagnostic logging disabled. Install it in place of `KOTOR.vpk` if you prefer not to have a log file written.

## Updating

Install `KOTOR.vpk` over your current one. Your files in `ux0:data/kotor/`, your saves and your mods stay as they are.

## Honest limits

- **A save that already lost Griff is not repaired.** The fix stops the problem from happening, but if you opened a Sand People chest before talking to Czerka in an existing save, Griff will still be missing in that save. Open an issue with the save attached and it can be fixed by hand.

- **32-bit textures use most of the Vita's video memory.** On Tatooine they ran at the same speed as 16-bit but left under 1 MB of video memory free, against about 29 MB with 16-bit. Past that point textures are kept in slower RAM. They have not yet been tried in the biggest areas, such as Taris and Dantooine.

- **The sound channel fix has not yet been tested in the fight where the repeating line was first heard,** the Sith base on Manaan. It ran cleanly everywhere it was played.

- **30 fps is a cap, not a promise.** Busy places, such as Taris's Upper City and Undercity with a crowd, or the Dantooine ruins, still drop to around 20 fps.

- Sith armour can still turn a bright solid colour.

## Everything else from v0.4.1 still applies

Frames are locked to 30 fps, grass stands still unless `GrassWind=1`, mods load from the memory card, and the MODS button switches between mod sets.

## You need your own copy of the game

This is a loader, not a game. It needs the Android release's `main` and `patch` `.obb` files, which you must own.

## Something broken?

Open an issue with `ux0:data/kotor/log.txt` attached, and say where in the game it happened.
